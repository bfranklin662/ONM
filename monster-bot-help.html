Game Rules

Playing

  • You need a signed-in account to play. Your progress saves to that account.
  • 501, straight in, double out. You throw first in leg one. The starting player alternates each leg after that.
  • BO1, BO3, BO5 and BO7 mean best of 1, 3, 5 and 7 legs: first to 1, 2, 3 or 4 legs wins.
  • Enter your three-dart score. Standard checkouts finish the leg immediately, with three darts recorded for the visit. Standard-mode busts score zero for that visit.
  • Green tick = a win. Each flawless challenge (no legs lost) earns one gold star. Replaying the same challenge cannot earn extra stars. Complete all five formats to complete a level.
  • Select a format pill, then use the results icon underneath to see players and their best scores. The leaderboard shows your collected gold stars.

Gold stars

  • Earn one gold star for each unique flawless challenge: win without losing a leg (1–0, 2–0, 3–0 or 4–0).
  • You can upgrade a previous green-tick win by beating that challenge flawlessly. Repeating a flawless win does not earn another star.
  • There are 50 stars to collect: five formats across ten bots. BO1 and Bull or Bust wins are automatically flawless.
  • Your total appears above the game and on the leaderboard. Stars are not spent when unlocking bots.
  • Required totals: Venton 6, Istan 10, Nick 14, Ricky 18, Aussie 22, Andre 26, Bailey 30 and Filip 34. You must also meet that bot's other requirements.
  • Bot cards stay their normal colour until all five modes are beaten, then turn green. All five flawless wins turn the card gold.

Bull or Bust

  • Unlock by beating BO1, BO3, BO5 and BO7 on this bot first. A single-leg match. You must finish on the Bullseye (50).
  • If your score falls below 50 without a Bull checkout, or you bust, you lose immediately.
  • When checking out, you must click Bullseye on the checkout prompt. Any other finish loses, and the Bull finish must be possible in three darts.
  • The bot plays normal double-out. A Bull or Bust win also earns a gold star because it is BO1.

Unlocking levels

  • Only unlocked levels and the next locked level are shown. The next locked bot stays blurred until unlocked and lists every requirement, with completed requirements ticked until the bot unlocks.
  • From Level 2: beat the previous bot in any two modes.
  • From Level 3: also beat BO1, BO3, BO5 and BO7 on the bot two levels below.
  • From Level 4: also beat all five formats on the bot three levels below.
  • From Level 5: also beat all five formats flawlessly on the bot four levels below.
  • Gold stars required: Venton 6, Istan 10, Nick 14, Ricky 18, Aussie 22, Andre 26, Bailey 30 and Filip 34. All requirements are cumulative. Bot averages are long-run targets; individual games vary.
