    Monster Bot Ladder rules

    Playing

    • You need a signed-in account to play. Your progress saves to that account.
    • 501, straight in, double out. You throw first in leg one. The starting player alternates each leg after that.
    • BO1, BO3, BO5 and BO7 mean best of 1, 3, 5 and 7 legs: first to 1, 2, 3 or 4 legs wins.
    • Enter your three-dart score. Standard checkouts finish the leg immediately, with three darts recorded for the visit. Standard-mode busts score zero for that visit.
    • Green tick = a win. Gold tick = a flawless win with no legs lost. Complete all five formats to complete a level.
    • Tap a format count to see the players who have won it and their best scores. Leaderboard flawless totals count each challenge once.

    Bull or Bust

    • Unlock by beating BO1, BO3, BO5 and BO7 on this bot first. A single-leg match. You must finish on the Bullseye (50).
    • If your score falls below 50 without a Bull checkout, or you bust, you lose immediately.
    • When checking out, you must click Bullseye on the checkout prompt. Any other finish loses, and the Bull finish must be possible in three darts.
    • The bot plays normal double-out. A Bull or Bust win also earns a gold tick because it is BO1.

    Unlocking levels

    • Only unlocked levels and the next locked level are shown. Each level lists its own requirements.
    • From Level 2: beat the previous bot in any two modes.
    • From Level 3: also beat BO1, BO3, BO5 and BO7 on the bot two levels below.
    • From Level 4: also beat all five formats on the bot three levels below.
    • From Level 5: also beat all five formats flawlessly on the bot four levels below.
    • All requirements are cumulative. Bot averages are long-run targets; individual games vary.
