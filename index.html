Welcome to the Oche Ness Monsters

Good darts. Questionable decisions.

A club with a taste for bull glory.

Founded by Nick Horne and Ricky Plows in 2023, we’re a London darts club built on competitive nights, good company and the belief that the next dart will definitely be better.

We’ve taken to the oche in the Banks, Trafalgar, Smithfield and COLDA leagues, as well as the Alex Beckett competition. Our own Monsters Premier League keeps the rivalries going when the regular season takes a breather.
